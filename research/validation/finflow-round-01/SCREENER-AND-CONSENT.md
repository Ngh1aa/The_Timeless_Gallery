# FinFlow Round 01 — Screener & Consent

## Internal study purpose
Evaluate comprehension of a simulated multi-rail settlement concept for MTO/PSP operations. Do not reveal preferred answers during recruitment.

## Screener

1. In the past 12 months, how often have you worked with business payment, payout, settlement, treasury or reconciliation workflows?
   - Never
   - Once or twice
   - Monthly
   - Weekly or more often

2. Which best describes your role in those workflows?
   - I initiate or operate payments/payouts
   - I monitor settlement/reconciliation
   - I approve or supervise payment operations
   - I troubleshoot payment/settlement exceptions
   - I design/build/support adjacent financial products but do not operate this workflow directly
   - Other

3. Which contexts have you worked with recently? Select any that apply.
   - Money transfer / remittance
   - Payment service provider / payment gateway
   - Cross-border payout operations
   - Treasury / liquidity operations
   - Reconciliation / settlement operations
   - Banking operations
   - Compliance / risk
   - None of these

4. When a payout cannot proceed through the expected path, have you personally had to investigate what happened or what should happen next?
   - Frequently
   - Occasionally
   - Never

5. Are there accessibility, assistive-technology, language, device or support needs we should plan for so you can participate comfortably?

## Classification

### DIRECT_USER
Classify as `DIRECT_USER` only when the participant has recent hands-on responsibility for a target settlement/payout/treasury/reconciliation workflow and can credibly discuss routing or exception handling from practice.

### PROXY
Classify adjacent banking operations, fintech product, finance, compliance or infrastructure participants as `PROXY` when they can provide useful domain context but do not directly perform the target workflow.

Do not upgrade a proxy participant after the session merely because their feedback is useful.

## Exclusions

- no relevant financial-operations exposure;
- participant would need to use real credentials or confidential transaction data to complete the study;
- cannot provide informed consent;
- direct conflict that makes the participant evaluate their own submitted work rather than the FinFlow concept.

## Consent script

Before the session, confirm:

- This is a portfolio/recruitment-test prototype using simulated data; it is not a live settlement system.
- Participation is voluntary and may stop at any time.
- The participant should not share real account credentials, customer information, confidential company data or real transaction screenshots.
- Notes may be stored in anonymized form and used to improve the prototype/case study.
- Any quote used publicly requires separate explicit permission; otherwise findings will be paraphrased.
- The participant may ask for a question or task to be skipped.

Record only:
- consent to participate: YES / NO;
- consent to anonymized notes: YES / NO;
- quote permission if requested separately: YES / NO / NOT REQUESTED.

Do not store names, email addresses, phone numbers or employer-sensitive information in the research repository.
