Project Overview

A 24-hour B2B FinTech design exercise focused on making multi-rail settlement understandable, inspectable and conversion-ready for MTO and PSP buyers.

Project snapshot

Design the story around the operating decision — not around fintech decoration.

The brief asks for a landing page and paid-social creative. The design response reframes the product as an inspectable orchestration layer: one payout instruction enters, five rails remain visible, one route is selected, and settlement state stays legible.

ProductMulti-Rail Settlement

Bank, wallet, card, stablecoin and cash payout routes under one orchestration layer.

Primary audienceMTO / PSP teams

Operations, product and payment stakeholders evaluating payout infrastructure.

Exercise format24-hour recruitment test

Research synthesis, UX framing, visual design, responsive direction and campaign creative.

Primary outcomeComprehension before conversion

Help a buyer understand the mechanism before asking for a workflow demo.

SignatureSettlement atlas + route desk

A dotted global settlement field and one inspectable routing object replace generic AI-fintech dashboard decoration.

Evidence policyNo fabricated proof

No invented customers, certifications, uptime, routing rules or performance numbers.

Challenge

Five rails increase flexibility, but they can also increase ambiguity.

The landing has to communicate technical infrastructure without becoming an API-documentation page, and sell confidence without pretending the prototype has production evidence it does not have.

Buyer question 01What does FinFlow actually do?

Show the product between instruction and settlement, not as a vague “payments platform.”

Buyer question 02Which rail gets used?

Keep all five rails visible and make selected / available / unavailable states explicit.

Buyer question 03Can I trust the system?

Build trust through inspectable behavior and traceability, not unsupported marketing claims.

Scope & constraints

What is inside the exercise — and what is deliberately left outside.

In scope

✓Desktop-first responsive landing page.
✓Interactive route / scenario concept.
✓Six-step settlement mechanism.
✓Trust, operating outcomes and demo conversion.
✓1080 × 1080 paid-social creative.

Not claimed / not in scope

—No live payment routing or production integration.
—No pricing, onboarding or authenticated dashboard flow.
—No fabricated compliance certification or SLA.
—No fake user-research results or conversion uplift.
—No claim that simulated routing logic is production logic.
Case-study stance

This pack distinguishes verified prototype behavior from design hypotheses. “PASS” is used only for checks actually run on the implemented site; user outcomes remain measurement plans until real usage exists.

Design objective

Operational · Editorial · Human-made

The redesigned direction uses a light institutional canvas, settlement-led artifacts and restrained motion so the product feels intentionally authored rather than generated from a generic fintech template.
