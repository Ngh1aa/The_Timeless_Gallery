Wireframe rules

What is intentionally absent at this fidelity.

RemovedWhyWhat remains
Brand imagery / logo assetVisual branding should not affect layout evaluation.Plain LOGO placeholder only.
Settlement atlas / halftone graphicsDecorative signature belongs to final visual design.Product-area boundary and route-desk footprint.
Semantic colorsSelected / available / unavailable colors are final UI decisions.Black, white and grayscale state emphasis.
Final shadow / elevationDepth should not make the wireframe feel polished.Simple 1px borders and flat surfaces.
Final copy stylingWireframe validates hierarchy, not art direction.Heading, body, label and CTA roles.
Fidelity definition

This is a structural wireframe of the current landing page, not a monochrome screenshot of the final design. The goal is to validate information hierarchy, section order, interaction zones and desktop-to-mobile transformation before color, imagery and brand craft are applied.
