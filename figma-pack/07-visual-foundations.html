Visual Foundations

The redesign uses a light institutional canvas, editorial Inter hierarchy and domain-specific settlement artifacts. Color is semantic first; elevation appears only where a transaction or route object needs to feel physically distinct.

Direction

Operational · Editorial · Human-made

The system intentionally moves away from generic dark fintech dashboards, glow-heavy AI aesthetics and repeated bento-card layouts.

SignatureSettlement atlas + route desk

Dotted global field, one payout instruction, one switch, five persistent rail states.

Surface modelNear-white canvas + white product objects

Dark navy is reserved for the settlement trace and primary actions.

CompositionLedger rows over card grids

Information hierarchy comes from alignment, borders and typography rather than decorative containers.
