Information Architecture

The campaign IA is deliberately shallow: a focused three-section narrative on the landing, supported by the ad creative and a direct workflow-demo path.

Page architecture

Three sections, each with one job in the evaluation journey.

01Hero / Control PlaneQuestion: What is FinFlow? · Proposition · scenario switcher · five rail states · workflow-demo CTA.
02Mechanism / Route InspectorQuestion: How does it work? · Receive → Analyze → FinFlow → Select & Route → Settle → Payout.
03Trust + Outcomes + ConversionQuestion: Why continue evaluation? · operating outcomes · settlement trace · evidence policy · demo request.
Content hierarchy

Information is ordered by decision dependency.

A buyer should not be asked to believe outcomes before understanding the mechanism, and should not be pushed to convert before the product model is clear.

L1

Positioning
