Desktop Wireframe

The desktop wireframe defines hierarchy, reading order and interaction ownership before visual polish. The route inspector is given the largest information surface because it carries both product explanation and differentiation.

Structure

Wireframe the argument before styling the interface.

The composition uses a 12-column mental model: compact product framing on the left, operational proof on the right, then a structured mechanism and final decision surface.

Frame annotations

Each region has one clear responsibility.

01 · Sticky nav

Orientation + fast conversion

Three anchors only: Mechanism, Trust & outcomes, Ad creative; demo CTA stays available without dominating the page.

02 · Hero copy

Category + mental model

Audience cue, headline and short explanation establish what FinFlow is before the user interacts.

03 · Control plane

Primary proof object

Largest hero region shows one instruction, FinFlow and five rails at once.

04 · Mechanism

Progressive technical depth

Six steps explain movement without exposing unvalidated production routing criteria.

05 · Trust surface

Credibility without fake proof

Operational outcomes and trace language replace unsupported logos, SLA claims or certifications.

06 · Final CTA

Workflow-specific action

Conversion is framed around bringing one payout journey, not a generic sales form.

07 · Light / dark rhythm

Separate learning modes

Dark surfaces represent live operational state; light surfaces support explanation and scanning.

08 · Repeated grammar

Reduce relearning

Route-state colors and terms recur in hero, mechanism, mobile and ad creative.

Layout decisions

Why the desktop silhouette looks this way.

W01Hero = copy 5 / system 7

The product is easier to believe when the operational model visually outweighs marketing copy.

Risk: dense UI; mitigated with restrained text and semantic state color.

W02Mechanism = navigation + inspector

Users can scan all six steps, then focus on one without losing sequence context.

Risk: too many interactions; keep only one current state.

W03Trust + conversion are combined

The final section answers “why continue?” and immediately provides the next action.

Risk: content crowding; use clear subregions instead of separate sections.

Wireframe criterion

If the page cannot explain FinFlow in grayscale without animation, the structure is not doing enough work. Motion is treated as reinforcement, not as the only way to understand the product.
