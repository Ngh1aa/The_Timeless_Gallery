Desktop annotations

What this wireframe validates.

01 · Hero hierarchy

Product message before details

Primary proposition and action occupy the left column; the route desk carries the interactive explanation.

02 · Product footprint

Route desk gets visual priority

The operational object is larger than supporting copy because product comprehension is the core proof.

03 · Rail visibility

All five paths remain visible

The selected rail is emphasized without removing alternatives from the mental model.

04 · Mechanism

Navigation + inspector

A persistent step list keeps sequence visible while the larger panel explains one state at a time.

05 · Operations

Rows, not feature cards

The operating model uses a ledger structure to reduce visual fragmentation.

06 · Conversion

Evidence before action

The settlement trace sits beside the form so the next step follows operational context.

Wireframe fidelity

Desktop wireframe uses only black, white and gray structural primitives. No image assets, SVGs, canvas graphics, brand color, final shadow, halftone atlas or production styling are allowed in this frame.
