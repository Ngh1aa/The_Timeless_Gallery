Success Metrics & Next Steps

Because FinFlow is a recruitment prototype, this frame defines what should be instrumented and learned after launch rather than inventing performance results. Metrics connect comprehension, engagement, trust and qualified demo intent.

Measurement model

Measure whether people understand the product before optimizing conversion.

A high CTA rate is not useful if users misunderstand what FinFlow does. The measurement tree therefore starts with comprehension and interaction quality, then moves toward qualified intent.

Product comprehension

Hero comprehensionCan the user identify audience, product role and core mechanism?
Route-state comprehensionCan the user identify selected / available / unavailable states?
Mechanism comprehensionCan the user explain the six-step journey in their own words?

Behavioral intent

Scenario interaction rateDo evaluators explore more than the default payout state?
Mechanism depthHow far do users engage with the six-step inspector?
Qualified demo progressionDo users who inspect the product continue to the workflow-demo action?

Friction signals

Early exitDrop-off before users reach the mechanism section.
Interaction abandonmentScenario or mechanism controls used once and immediately abandoned.
CTA confusionClicks or user-test feedback indicating unclear demo expectations.

Counter-metrics

MisinterpretationUsers believe the simulated route reflects real production logic.
OveremphasisUsers think FinFlow is primarily a stablecoin product.
Accessibility regressionsInteraction growth should not come at the cost of keyboard, motion or readability quality.

Next iteration loop

Research → instrument → observe → revise.

01

User-test terminology

Validate “rail,” “orchestration” and “workflow demo” with relevant roles.

02

Validate routing context

Identify which inputs operators expect to see before trusting a route selection.

03

Instrument events

Track scenario, mechanism and CTA behavior with source context.

04

Establish baseline

Observe comprehension and funnel behavior before setting targets.

05

Prioritize evidence

Add proof that users actually need rather than generic social proof.

06

Revise the system

Update content, state model and interaction based on observed failures.

Outcome status

The current project demonstrates design reasoning and implemented interaction quality. It does not claim business lift, user success or production readiness until those outcomes are measured with real evidence.
