Problem

Multi-rail flexibility creates an operating visibility problem.

MTO and PSP teams may have several possible payout paths, but a landing page still needs to make three things obvious: where FinFlow sits, which rail is selected and how the transaction progresses after that decision.

Insights

Six principles connect the research to the interface.

01Complexity should stay visible — but organized.

All five rails remain visible while the active route is emphasized.

02Trust can come from inspectability.

Visible state and trace context are more credible than invented proof.

03Mechanism belongs in the value proposition.

The routing model appears in the hero, not hidden deep on the page.

04Domain objects should drive the visual language.

Ledger rows, transaction slips and route lines replace generic SaaS cards.

05Conversion should be workflow-specific.

“Bring one payout journey” is more concrete than a generic sales CTA.

06Evidence boundaries should be visible.

Simulation, planned validation and unknown business outcomes are labeled as such.
