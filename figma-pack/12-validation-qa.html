Usability validation plan

What still needs real users before making outcome claims.

This study is planned, not claimed as completed.

01

Recruit

Target payment operations, PSP product or related fintech infrastructure roles.

02

First impression

5-second comprehension: who is this for and what does FinFlow do?

03

Route task

Ask participants to explain which rail is selected and what alternatives remain.

04

Mechanism task

Have them narrate the six-step flow in their own words.

05

Trust probe

Ask what increases or reduces confidence in the product story.

06

CTA test

Assess whether “workflow demo” feels clear, useful and credible.

Research questions

What the next study should answer.

RQ01Can users explain FinFlow’s role after the hero?

Look for “orchestration layer across rails,” not just “payments company.”

RQ02Are rail states unambiguous?

Participants should distinguish selected, available and unavailable without guidance.

RQ03Does the six-step mechanism reduce uncertainty?

Test whether the sequence clarifies the settlement journey or adds cognitive load.

RQ04Does inspectability create trust?

Identify which visible states matter and what proof is still missing.

RQ05Is stablecoin proportionate in the story?

Ensure it reads as one rail, not as the main product narrative.

RQ06Is the CTA meaningful?

Determine whether buyers understand what they would bring to the demo.
