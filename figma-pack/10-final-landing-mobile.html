Mobile-specific quality rules

What the responsive version must protect.

Rule 01No hover dependency

All critical information and actions are visible or tappable.

Rule 02No clipped operational labels

Rail names, states and transaction context must wrap or recompose safely.

Rule 03State before animation

Selected text/border changes remain understandable even when motion is reduced.

Rule 04Thumb-safe controls

Tabs and CTAs use sufficient spacing instead of desktop micro-controls.

Rule 05Shorter copy blocks

Technical explanation is divided into digestible segments.

Rule 06Same terminology

Mobile does not rename states or steps just to fit the viewport.
