Key design decisions

What changed from a typical fintech landing pattern.

UI01No abstract hero illustration

The operational interface itself becomes the visual signature.

Benefit: visual distinctiveness also improves comprehension.

UI02Only one accent family

Cobalt highlights route selection; other states use muted semantic colors.

Benefit: hierarchy stays technical instead of promotional.

UI03Minimal card grammar

Large structural surfaces replace a page made of interchangeable bento cards.

Benefit: stronger narrative continuity.

UI04Motion follows state

Semantic rail state changes before path animation begins.

Benefit: animation explains cause and effect instead of acting as spectacle.
