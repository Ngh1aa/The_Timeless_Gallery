04Trust & control

Financial infrastructure earns trust by exposing the state of the system.

FinFlow’s concept keeps route choice, transaction state and operational context reviewable without pretending to replace an MTO or PSP’s existing risk controls.

01Compliance-ready fit

Fit existing AML/KYC workflows rather than replace them. Final policy and control scope depends on implementation.

02Audit-ready traceability

Preserve rail selection and settlement state as a reviewable transaction history for post-transaction investigation.

03Data-control posture

Minimize unnecessary data movement. Encryption, residency and retention require product and security validation.

04Transparent operations

Keep rail state, selected path and routing context visible rather than hiding the decision behind a black box.
